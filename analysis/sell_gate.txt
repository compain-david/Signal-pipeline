SELL GATE - measurability audit and T2 backtest
====================================================================

SHADOW ONLY. This gate governs nothing, exactly like the ladder.

1. CAN THE GATE FIRE?   No. It never could.
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  lth_distribution_30d         UNMEASURABLE
      rule : long-term-holder supply falling for 30 days or more
      why  : no free LTH supply series; 4 BGeometrics endpoints 404 (see LTH_ENDPOINTS_TRIED_404). Glassnode/CryptoQuant are paid.
  sth_rp_weekly_loss           MEASURABLE
      rule : BTC weekly close below short-term-holder realised price
      why  : sth_realized_price is in analysis/series.json; BTC price is derived from CoinMetrics CapMrktCurUSD / SplyCur.
  etf_flow_reversal_near_ath   UNMEASURABLE
      rule : ETF net flows turn negative while within 10% of the ATH
      why  : no public ETF flow API. The 'within 10% of ATH' half IS computable, but the flow reversal carries the signal and it is not readable - a half-condition does not vote.

  The rule is 2 of 3 within 60 days. Readable inputs: 1 of 3.
  1 < 2, so the threshold is unreachable by construction. The only
  mechanism in this framework that protects capital cannot fire, and
  no run has ever reported that.

  Endpoints tried for T1, all 404: lth-supply, long-term-holder-supply, lth-net-position-change, lth-sth-supply

2. WHAT THE GATE DOES IN THAT STATE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  Denominator policy: fixed. Required stays 2 of 3.

  Rejected alternative - shrink the denominator, as the rotation gate
  and the ladder do. There it is safe: a thin base makes them abstain,
  and abstention costs opportunity. Here, 2 of 3 rescaled onto 1
  readable input means ONE signal exits the entire book - and the
  surviving signal is the noisiest of the three, per section 3.

  Also rejected - fire nothing and stay silent. That is the default
  behaviour of a 2-of-3 counter with 1 input, and it renders as 'no
  sell signal', which a reader cannot distinguish from an all-clear.

  What it does instead: four distinct verdicts, so blindness can never
  be read as calm.
    FIRE         threshold met - de-risk per the strategy
    ESCALATE     signals fired but the threshold is unreachable with the inputs that exist - a human must judge this, the gate cannot
    BLIND_QUIET  nothing fired, but the gate could not have fired even in principle - this is NOT an all-clear
    QUIET        nothing fired, and the gate was able to fire - a real all-clear

  Today, with only T2 readable: T2 quiet -> BLIND_QUIET, T2 firing ->
  ESCALATE. Never FIRE. Not a sale, not a silence: a human decision,
  logged as owed.

3. T2 BACKTEST (the only computable signal)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  Window        : 2022-08-31 -> 2026-08-28
  Weekly closes : 209
  T2 fired      : 86 weeks (41.1% of all weekly closes)
  Episodes      : 7 clusters more than 28 days apart

  That firing rate is what settles the denominator question. A
  capital-protection trigger that is on nearly half of all weeks
  cannot be allowed to act alone, whatever else is unavailable.

  BTC forward return, horizon 30 days
    bucket                           n     median  positive
    T2 fired (every week)           83       2.0%       63%
    T2 fired (1st of episode)        7       4.0%       71%   n<30, not readable
    BASELINE (every week)          204       1.7%       56%
    edge vs baseline, every week    +0.3 points

  BTC forward return, horizon 60 days
    bucket                           n     median  positive
    T2 fired (every week)           79       2.4%       57%
    T2 fired (1st of episode)        7       1.2%       57%   n<30, not readable
    BASELINE (every week)          200       2.6%       56%
    edge vs baseline, every week    -0.3 points

  BTC forward return, horizon 90 days
    bucket                           n     median  positive
    T2 fired (every week)           75       9.1%       59%
    T2 fired (1st of episode)        7      -0.0%       43%   n<30, not readable
    BASELINE (every week)          196       7.0%       58%
    edge vs baseline, every week    +2.1 points

  Overlapping windows are NOT independent observations: 209 weekly
  closes contain on the order of 16 independent 90-day windows.
  Read the sign, not the decimal.

  Reading it: no edge, and the little there is points the wrong way
  for a sell trigger. Median BTC return after a T2 week is POSITIVE at
  all three horizons and at or above the baseline. Selling on T2 alone
  would mostly have sold into recoveries. The episode bucket (n=7)
  is printed for completeness only; that many clusters cannot separate
  skill from luck at any horizon.

4. T2 AND T3 BARELY CO-EXIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  Drawdown from the running ATH when T2 fired:
    median 45.3%    min 11.6%    max 75.9%
    firings within 10% of the ATH: 0 of 86

  T2 firing a median 45% off the highs, never once within 10% of them,
  says what T2 actually is: a capitulation marker, not a distribution
  marker. That is an argument for keeping it as one vote of three, and
  the opposite of an argument for promoting it to one of one.

  T3 requires being within 10% of the ATH; T2 fires deep in drawdowns.
  Even with a working ETF feed the pair would rarely co-occur, so the
  2-of-3 rule leans on T1 far harder than its symmetric phrasing
  admits. Sourcing LTH supply is the highest-value fix available.

5. LIMITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  - STH-RP history on disk starts 2022-08-31: 209 weeks inside one
    cycle. Small for a signal meant to fire once or twice per cycle.
  - Overlapping windows; in-sample; descriptive, not predictive.
  - T1 and T3 appear in no table above. They are not zero, not
    neutral, not proxied - they are unmeasured.
  - BTC USD is derived CapMrktCurUSD / SplyCur, a daily UTC close; it
    will not tick-match an exchange print.

6. WHAT WOULD CHANGE THE ANSWER
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  One sourced LTH supply series makes the gate operable: 2 of 3 becomes
  reachable, and T1 is the signal T2 most needs beside it - slow,
  structural, and not a drawdown artefact. That is one purchase, not a
  redesign. Until it exists the correct output is ESCALATE or
  BLIND_QUIET, never a number that looks like a decision.
