2. WHAT THE GATE DOES IN THAT STATE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  Denominator policy: fixed. Required stays 2 of 3 whatever is readable.

  FLAGGED FOR THE OWNER: this overrides an explicit instruction. The
  brief said 'if a data series does not exist, say so and reduce the
  denominator'. The saying-so is done throughout; the reducing is
  refused, for the reason below. Overrule it by setting
  DENOMINATOR_POLICY - it is one line, and the tests say which ones
  break. Raised here so it is a decision, not a discovery.

  Rejected alternative - shrink the denominator, as the rotation gate
  and the ladder do. There it is safe: a thin base makes them abstain,
  and abstention costs opportunity. Here, 2 of 3 rescaled onto 1
  readable input asks for 1 - and the signal that survived is the
  noisiest of the three, per section 3.
  At 1, one signal exits the entire book.

  Also rejected - fire nothing and stay silent. That is the default
  behaviour of a 2-of-3 counter with 1 input, and it renders as 'no
  sell signal', which a reader cannot distinguish from an all-clear.

  What it does instead: 5 distinct verdicts, so neither blindness nor
  a half-met threshold can be read as calm.
    FIRE         threshold met - de-risk per the strategy
    ARMED        a signal fired, the threshold was not met, the gate was able to fire - one more distinct signal in the window would fire it
    ESCALATE     signals fired but the threshold is unreachable with the inputs that exist - a human must judge this, the gate cannot
    BLIND_QUIET  nothing fired, but the gate could not have fired even in principle - this is NOT an all-clear
    QUIET        nothing fired, and the gate was able to fire - a real all-clear

  Reachable outcomes with 1 of 3 inputs readable, computed by
  reachable_verdicts() rather than asserted here: ESCALATE, BLIND_QUIET.
  Never FIRE. Not a sale and not a silence: a human decision,
  logged as owed.
  Section 0 is that mapping actually executed on the current data.

  case is that a signal present on 41.1% of weeks must not act
  alone - and it then routes that same signal to a human on
  67.9% of weeks. An escalation raised two weeks in three is not
  an escalation; it is a standing condition, and a human asked to

  the 2-of-3 rule has to fix this, not inherit it.

4. T2 AND T3 BARELY CO-EXIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  Drawdown from the running ATH when T2 fired:
    median 45.3%    min 11.6%    max 75.9%

  T3's '10% below the ATH' is the owner's threshold. The whole
  co-occurrence conclusion rests on it, so here are its neighbours:
    threshold   firings within it
        10%      0 of 86
        12%      1 of 86
        15%      5 of 86
        20%      12 of 86

  Read that before quoting the 0. The nearest firing was 11.6% off
  the high - 1.6 points from counting. 'Never' is true at the 10%
  definition and false by 20%; the finding is real but it is not
  robust to the threshold, and a 15% T3 would change the answer.

  So, stated at the precision the data supports: T2 and T3 never
  co-occurred at the 10% definition; the nearest firing was 11.6%
  off the high. T2 fires a median 45% off the highs, which says
  what T2 actually is - a capitulation marker, not a distribution
  marker. That is an argument for keeping it as one vote of three,
  and the opposite of an argument for promoting it to one of one.

  T3 requires being near the ATH; T2 fires deep in drawdowns. Even with
  a working ETF feed the pair would rarely co-occur, so the 2-of-3 rule
  leans on T1 far harder than its symmetric phrasing admits. Sourcing
  LTH supply is the highest-value fix available.

5. LIMITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  - STH-RP history on disk starts 2022-09-01: 209 weeks inside one cycle.
    Small for a signal meant to fire once or twice per cycle.
  - ASSET MISMATCH. T2 is a BTC rule, T3 is specified on ETH/BTC ETF
    flows, and the book is an ETH/BTC rotation. Section 3 measures the
    cost of selling in BTC terms, section 3B in book terms; neither is
    the whole answer, and the gate itself has never said which asset
    it is protecting. That is an unresolved specification gap, not a
    reporting choice - the owner has to name the book.
  - The newest row on the weekly grid is a PARTIAL week (2026-08-28, a Friday;
    the week ends 2026-08-30). Section 0's live verdict is computed on it.
  - Overlapping windows; in-sample; descriptive, not predictive.
  - T1 and T3 appear in no table anywhere. They are not zero, not
    neutral, not proxied - they are unmeasured.
  - BTC USD is derived CapMrktCurUSD / SplyCur, a daily UTC close; it
    will not tick-match an exchange print.
  - No BGeometrics endpoint was probed on this run. The T1 404 is
    quoted from README:146, not measured here.

6. WHAT WOULD CHANGE THE ANSWER
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  1 more sourced input makes the gate operable: 2 of 3 becomes
  reachable, and LTH supply is the one to buy first - T1 is slow,
  structural, and not a drawdown artefact, which is exactly what T2
  is not. That is one purchase, not a redesign. Until it exists the
  correct output is ESCALATE or BLIND_QUIET, never a number that looks
  like a decision.
