   HOW eth_btc_momentum IS RECONSTRUCTED HERE - read before trusting any MOM cell below
   ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
     This study computes it as the percent change from t-14 CALENDAR days to t, straight off
     analysis/ethbtc.json. The LIVE fetcher instead takes CoinGecko's last 14 DAILY POINTS.
     Same rule, different bytes: the builder of ethbtc.json is not in this repo, so its venue and its
     close convention are unverified here, and a missing day shifts CoinGecko's window but not this one.
     Consequence: a reading within a point or two of the 10.0% threshold is AMBIGUOUS, not decided.
     Readings far from the threshold are safe; the FIRE/. call on a near-threshold day is not.

6. COVERAGE FLOOR CHECK - does ladder.COVERAGE_FLOOR (70%) hold on ANY day of 2021?
======================================================================================================================
   days examined                           365 (all of 2021)
   ladder coverage   min .. max            14.3% .. 14.3%
   days at or above the 70% floor         0
   days the gate could reach 5 of 8       0

   The floor holds on ZERO days. The ladder would have been FROZEN for the whole of 2021, including
   every day of both altseasons. next_state() would have returned
   "frozen - coverage 14.29% below the 70% floor" 365 times and never left its starting rung.

   This matters more than any individual signal. A design whose safety rule is 'refuse to act on a
   thin base' will refuse to act for an entire cycle if the base was thin for an entire cycle - and
   the ladder's own docstring priced that at 49.2% of days frozen under a 15% outage. 2021 is the
   100%-outage case it never modelled.

======================================================================================================================
7. CONCLUSION
======================================================================================================================

   The current signal set CANNOT be evaluated against the 2021 altseasons. 7 of its 8 Tier A signals
   have no readable value on any date in either window. Coverage is 12.5%, against a gate that needs 5 of 8
   to fire and a ladder that needs 70% to act at all. Both fail on the inputs, not on the market.

   Three consequences, in increasing order of how much they matter:

   (a) The gate never fires in 2021 - but that is not a result. With at most 1 scoreable signals against
       a threshold of 5, 'did not fire' carries no information about 2021 whatsoever. Nobody should
       read the empty column as the gate correctly staying out.

   (b) The ladder is frozen every day of the year, so ladder.py's Monte Carlo has never been exposed to
       a 2021-shaped move. It is block-bootstrapped from 1366 real days starting 2022-11-29 - derived here
       by running montecarlo.to_votes over analysis/series.json, the same input the Monte Carlo reads.
       Its 0.00 whipsaws/yr is a property of the 14-day dwell, not evidence it survives an altseason
       it has never seen.

   (c) One Tier A signal, eth_etf_flows, is PERMANENTLY unscoreable against 2021: US spot ETH ETFs did
       not exist until 2024-07-23. No backfill fixes that. Any future claim that this framework 'would
       have caught 2021' is capped at 7 of 8 signals before the work even starts.

   What is NOT claimed here: that the thresholds are wrong. This study cannot tell, and does not guess.
   It says only that the evidence usually cited for them - two 2021 episodes - is evidence this signal
   set has never been tested against, and that the calibration rests on memory rather than measurement.

   The cheapest fix is bounded: a BGeometrics backfill of MVRV Z, NVT, SSR and STH-RP to 2021, plus a
   daily BTC close for the STH comparison. At 10 requests/hour that is hours of waiting, not weeks of
   work. What it would buy is MEASURED here, not estimated: the same availability scan is re-run against
   a COPY of series.json filled with placeholder values across both windows and the 90 prior days NVT's
   average needs. Only the SHAPE of that history decides the answer; no placeholder value is printed,
   scored, or carried into any figure above. An earlier version of this report asserted a number here
   that the module itself refuted, which is why it is computed now.

     scoreable Tier A signals today                 1 of 8
     after the backfill above                       5 of 8   (gained: mvrv_z_score, nvt, stablecoin_supply_ratio, sth_realized_price)
     gate threshold                                 5 of 8 needed  ->  REACHABLE
     best ladder coverage after the backfill        28.6% vs the 70% floor  ->  STILL FROZEN, the floor stays untestable

   Read that second pair carefully: the prescribed backfill is a GATE fix, not a ladder fix. Of the four
   series it adds, only SSR sits on the rotation axis, so ladder coverage rises but stays under the
   70% floor and the ladder would still have been frozen through both altseasons. Making the floor
   testable against 2021 needs the rotation-only signals - dominance history and a funding archive -
   which is the harder and possibly unpurchasable half.

   Funding, netflow and dominance archives are the half that may not be purchasable at any price. If they
   are not, the spec should say so, rather than leaving them standing as assumed evidence.
